A neighbourhood chair you can trust

We started New Bombay for the people who live around Pandeypur Chauraha and Lalpur Road. The idea was simple: listen first, cut second. Every customer gets the style that fits their hair and face.

Many of our regulars first came for one haircut and now bring their brothers, fathers and friends. When a wedding is near, they come to us for the full grooming.
